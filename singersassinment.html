Top 10 Famous Singers In India

LEGENDRY ICONS

1. Lata Mangeshkar

Known as the Nightingale of India,
she recorded tens of thousands of songs across many languages and received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honor.

Full Name

Lata Deenanath Mangeshkar

Indroduction

Lata Mangeshkar was one of India's most legendary playback singers, known as the Nightingale of India.
She sang thousands of songs in several Indian languages and received the Bharat Ratna in 2001 for her outstanding contribution to Indian music.

Date Of Birth

(28 September 1929 – 6 February 2022)

Awards

Filmpire Award, National Award,Padma Bhusan And More many awards she Has.

Famous Songs

Lag Jaa Gale , Ajeeb Dastaan Hai Yeh , Tere Bina Zindagi Se , Pyar Kiya To Darna Kya and many more

2. Mohammad Rafi

Celebrated for his incredible vocal range and versatility, he sang classic romantic, patriotic, and devotional tracks.

Full Name

Mohammed Rafi

Indroduction

Mohammed Rafi was one of India's greatest playback singers, known for his melodious voice and versatility.
He sang many unforgettable songs in Hindi films and received the Padma Shri award in 1967 for his contribution to Indian music.

Date Of Birth

(24 December 1924 – 31 July 1980)

Awards

1967 – Honoured with the Padma Shri by the Government of India.
1948 – Rafi received a silver medal from the Indian Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru, on the first anniversary of the Indian Independence Day And More Awards

Famous Songs

Chaudhvin Ka Chand Ho , Baharon Phool Barsao , Likhe Jo Khat Tujhe , Kya Hua Tera Wada And many more

3. Kishor Kumar

A legendary playback singer and actor known for his unique yodelling style and expressive energy.

Full Name

Indroduction

(4 August 1929 – 13 October 1987)

Awards

Mere Sapno Ki Rani , Roop Tera Mastana , Zindagi Ek Safar Hai Suhana , Pal Pal Dil Ke Paas and many more
