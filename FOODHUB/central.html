Indori Poha

A Light tasty breakfast make with
flatterd rise spice and cruchy topping.

Ingredients : Poha , Onion , Mustard Seeds , Greeen Chillies , Curvy Leaves , Turmeric
Peanuts , Lemon , Sev.

Racipe : 1.Rinse The poha gently and let it soften
2.Heat oil and add mustard seeds , Peanuts , Curvy leaves and green chillies.
3.Add Choped onion and turmeric.
4.Mixed Poha and salt then cook for a few minuts.
5.Serve with lemon juice Serve coriander and sev

Dal Bafla

A traditional wheat based dish served with spiced

Ingredients: Wheat flour, semolina (optional), ghee, salt, toor dal, cumin, garlic and spices.

Racipe : 1.Knead flour, salt and a little ghee with water.
2.Shape the dough into round balls.
3.Boil the balls until nearly cooked, then bake until golden.
4.Cook toor dal with turmeric and salt.
5.Prepare a tempering with cumin, garlic and spices.
6.Serve the bafla with dal and ghee.

Bhutte Ka Kees

Famous in Indore, Madhya Pradesh
A creamy, savoury dish made from grated corn and aromatic spices.

Ingredients: Fresh corn, milk, mustard seeds, green chillies, turmeric, ginger, lemon and coriander.

Racipe : 1.Grate fresh corn kernels.
2.Heat oil and add mustard seeds, ginger and green chillies.
3.Add the grated corn and turmeric.
4.Pour in milk and cook while stirring until thick.
5.Add salt and lemon juice, then garnish with coriander.

Malpua

Popular in Madhya Pradesh and surrounding regions
A sweet, soft-centred pancake often served during festivals.

Ingredients : Flour, milk, sugar, fennel seeds, cardamom and ghee.

Racipe :1.Mix flour, milk, sugar and cardamom into a smooth batter.
2.Add fennel seeds and let the batter rest briefly.
3.Pour small portions into a lightly heated pan with ghee.
4.Fry until golden on both sides.
5.Dip briefly in warm sugar syrup if desired.

Bafauri

Origin: Chhattisgarh
A traditional steamed snack made with chana dal and spices.

Ingredients : Chana dal, onion, green chillies, ginger, coriander, cumin and salt.

Racipe : 1.Soak chana dal for several hours and drain.
2.Grind coarsely with ginger and green chillies.
3.Mix with chopped onion, coriander, cumin and salt.
4.Shape the mixture into small balls.
5.Steam until firm and cooked through.
6.Serve with green chutney.

Mawa Bati

Popular in Madhya Pradesh
A rich festive sweet made with milk solids and sugar syrup.

Ingredients : Mawa (khoya), flour, a little baking soda, sugar, water, cardamom and ghee.

Racipe : 1.Mix mawa with flour and a little baking soda.
2.Shape into small balls without cracks.
3.Fry over low to medium heat until evenly golden.
4.Prepare sugar syrup with water and cardamom.
5.Soak the fried balls in warm syrup before serving.
