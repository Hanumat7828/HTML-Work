West Indian Foods
Dhokla

Origin: Gujarat
A soft, spongy steamed snack made from gram flour.

Ingredients: Besan, yogurt, lemon juice, turmeric, fruit salt (ENO), mustard seeds, curry leaves and green chillies.

Recipe: 1. Mix besan, yogurt, water, salt and turmeric into a smooth batter.
2. Add lemon juice and fruit salt just before steaming.
3. Pour into a greased tray and steam for 15–20 minutes.
4. Heat oil and add mustard seeds, curry leaves and green chillies.
5. Pour the tempering over the dhokla and cut into squares.

Vada Pav

Origin: Maharashtra
A famous Mumbai street food consisting of a spiced potato fritter inside a bun.

Ingredients: Potatoes, pav buns, besan, mustard seeds, curry leaves, garlic, green chillies and spices.

Recipe: 1. Boil and mash potatoes.
2. Prepare a tempering with mustard seeds, curry leaves, garlic and chillies.
3. Mix the tempering into the potatoes and shape into balls.
4. Dip the balls in seasoned besan batter and deep-fry until golden.
5. Place inside pav buns with chutneys and serve.

Dal Baati Churma

Origin: Rajasthan
A traditional combination of baked wheat balls, lentil curry and sweetened wheat crumble.

Ingredients: Wheat flour, semolina, ghee, toor dal, moong dal, cumin, jaggery and cardamom.

Recipe: 1. Knead wheat flour, salt, ghee and water into a firm dough.
2. Shape into balls and bake until golden and cooked through.
3. Cook the lentils with turmeric, salt and spices.
4. Prepare a cumin and garlic tempering and add it to the dal.
5. Crush some baked baati with ghee, powdered sugar or jaggery and cardamom to make churma.
6. Serve the baati with dal and churma.

Goan Fish Curry

Origin: Goa
A tangy, spicy curry made with fish, coconut and regional spices.

Ingredients: Fish, coconut milk or grated coconut, red chillies, turmeric, coriander seeds, tamarind, onion and garlic.

Recipe: 1. Grind coconut, chillies, coriander seeds and garlic into a paste.
2. Sauté chopped onion in a pan.
3. Add the spice paste, tamarind and water or coconut milk.
4. Simmer the curry and add the fish.
5. Cook gently until the fish is fully cooked.
6. Serve with steamed rice.

Thepla

Origin: Gujarat
A spiced flatbread, often made with fresh fenugreek leaves.

Ingredients: Wheat flour, fenugreek leaves, yogurt, turmeric, chilli powder, cumin, salt and oil.

Recipe: 1. Mix flour, chopped fenugreek leaves, yogurt and spices.
2. Knead into a soft dough.
3. Divide into balls and roll into thin flatbreads.
4. Cook on a hot tawa with a little oil.
5. Serve with yogurt or pickle.
