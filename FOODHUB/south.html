South Indian Foods
Masala Dosa

Origin: Karnataka
A crispy fermented rice-and-lentil crepe filled with spiced potatoes.

Ingredients: Rice, urad dal, fenugreek seeds, potatoes, onion, mustard seeds and curry leaves.

Racipe : 1.Soak rice and urad dal separately, then grind them into a batter.
2.Ferment the batter overnight.
3.Prepare potato masala with onions and spices.
4.Spread batter thinly on a hot greased tawa.
5.Cook until crisp, add potato masala and fold.

Idli Sambar

Popular across South India
Soft, steamed rice cakes served with a tangy lentil-and-vegetable soup.

Ingredients: Idli rice, urad dal, toor dal, vegetables, tamarind, sambar powder and curry leaves.

Racipe : 1.Soak, grind and ferment the rice and urad dal batter.
2.Pour the batter into greased idli moulds.
3.Steam for around 10–15 minutes, until cooked.
4.Cook toor dal with vegetables, tamarind and sambar spices.
5.Serve the hot idlis with sambar and chutney.

Medu Vada

Popular in Tamil Nadu and Karnataka
Crispy outside and soft inside, these savoury lentil fritters are shaped like rings.

Ingredients :Urad dal, green chilli, ginger, curry leaves, black pepper and salt

Recipe:1.Soak urad dal for 3–4 hours.
2.Grind with very little water into a thick batter.
3.Mix in chopped chilli, ginger and curry leaves.
4.Shape into rings with a hole in the centre.
5.Deep-fry until golden and fully cooked.

Mysore Pak

Origin: Mysuru, Karnataka
A traditional sweet made from gram flour, sugar and ghee.

Ingredients : Gram flour (besan), sugar, ghee and water.

Recipe:1.Lightly roast gram flour to remove its raw smell.
2.Prepare a sugar syrup with water.
3.Gradually mix in the gram flour, stirring to prevent lumps.
4.Add warm ghee little by little while stirring continuously.
5.When the mixture thickens and releases some ghee, transfer it to a greased tray.
6.Let it set slightly and cut into squares.

Hyderabadi Biryani

Origin: Hyderabad, Telangana
A fragrant rice dish layered with aromatic spices, herbs and marinated vegetables or meat.

Ingredients: Basmati rice, vegetables or chicken, yogurt, onions, mint, coriander, biryani spices and saffron (optional).

Racipe : 1.Partially cook the basmati rice with whole spices.
2.Prepare a spiced yogurt marinade and cook the vegetables or chicken.
3.Layer the rice and filling in a heavy pot.
4.Cover tightly and cook over low heat until the rice and filling are fully cooked.
5.Garnish with mint and fried onions.
