North-Eastern Indian Foods
Momos

Popular in Sikkim

Ingredients : Flour, cabbage, carrot, onion, ginger, garlic, salt and pepper.

Racipe : 1.Knead flour and water into a soft dough.
2.Mix finely chopped vegetables with spices.
3.Roll the dough into small circles and add the filling.
4.Fold and seal the dumplings.

5.Steam for 10–15 minutes, until fully cooked.

Thukpa

Popular in Arunachal Pradesh

Ingredients: Noodles, cabbage, carrot, onion, ginger, garlic, vegetable stock and soy sauce.

Recipe: 1. Boil the noodles and set aside.
2. Sauté onion, ginger and garlic.
3. Add chopped vegetables and cook briefly.
4. Pour in vegetable stock and season.
5. Add noodles and simmer for a few minutes.

Eromba

Origin: Manipur

Ingredients: Potatoes, seasonal vegetables, green chillies, salt and optional fermented fish.

Recipe: 1. Boil potatoes and vegetables until soft.
2. Cook the chillies according to your preferred spice level.
3. Mash the vegetables and chillies together.
4. Add fermented fish if desired.
5. Mix well and serve with steamed rice.

Masor Tenga

Origin: Assam
A light fish curry with a distinctive tangy flavour.

Ingredients: Fish, tomatoes, lemon, turmeric, mustard oil and salt.

Recipe: 1. Season fish with salt and turmeric.
2. Lightly fry the fish in mustard oil.
3. Cook tomatoes until soft.
4. Add water and lemon juice to create a tangy broth.
5. Add the fish and simmer until fully cooked

Smoked Pork with Bamboo Shoots

Popular in Nagaland

Ingredients: Smoked pork, bamboo shoots, ginger, garlic, green chillies and salt.

Recipe: 1. Cut the pork into small pieces.
2. Cook with ginger, garlic and a little water.
3. Add prepared bamboo shoots and chillies.
4. Simmer until the pork is tender and thoroughly cooked.
5. Serve hot with rice.
