Chole Bhature

Origin: Punjab / North India
Spicy chickpea curry served with fluffy, deep-fried bhature.

ingredients : Chickpeas, onion, tomato, ginger-garlic paste, chole masala, flour, curd and oil.

Racipe : 1.Soak chickpeas overnight and pressure-cook until soft.
2.Cook onion, tomato and spices in a pan.
3.Add chickpeas and simmer for 10–15 minutes.
4.Knead flour with curd, let it rest, roll and fry the bhature.


Ingredients And Quantity

Dal Makhni

Origin: Punjab
A creamy, rich lentil dish commonly served with naan or rice

Ingredients: Whole black lentils (urad dal), kidney beans, tomato, butter, cream, ginger, garlic and spices.

Recipe:1.Soak lentils and kidney beans overnight.
2.Pressure-cook until soft.
3.Prepare a tomato, ginger and garlic masala.
4.Add the cooked lentils and simmer until thick.
5.Finish with butter and a little cream.

Paneer Butter Masala

Popular across North India
Soft paneer cubes in a smooth, buttery tomato gravy.

Ingredients: Paneer, tomatoes, onion, butter, cream, ginger-garlic paste and garam masala.

Racipe : 1.Cook tomatoes and onions with spices.
2.Blend into a smooth gravy.
3.Add butter, paneer cubes and a little water.
4.Simmer for 5–8 minutes and finish with cream.

Gajar Ka Halwa

Popular North Indian dessert
A sweet carrot pudding commonly prepared during winter.

Ingredients: Carrots, milk, sugar, ghee, cardamom and chopped nuts.

Recipe:1.Grate fresh carrots.
2.Cook them with milk until the milk reduces.
3.Add sugar and stir until the mixture thickens.
4.Mix in ghee, cardamom and nuts.

Kashmiri Rogan Josh

Origin: Kashmir
An aromatic red curry traditionally made with lamb or mutton.

Ingredients: Mutton, yogurt, Kashmiri chilli powder, fennel powder, dry ginger and whole spices.

Racipe :1.Brown the mutton with whole spices.
2.Add yogurt gradually while stirring.
3.Mix in chilli, fennel and dry ginger.
4.Add water and cook slowly until the meat becomes tender.
