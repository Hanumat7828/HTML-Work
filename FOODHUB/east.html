East Indian Foods
Shorshe Ilish

Origin: West Bengal
A traditional Bengali hilsa fish curry cooked in a flavourful mustard sauce.

Ingredients: Hilsa fish, mustard seeds, green chillies, turmeric, mustard oil and salt.

Recipe: 1. Rub fish with turmeric and salt.
2. Grind mustard seeds with green chillies and water to make a paste.
3. Heat mustard oil and lightly fry the fish.
4. Add the mustard paste and a little water.
5. Simmer gently until the fish is fully cooked.

Luchi and Cholar Dal

Origin: West Bengal
Fluffy fried flatbreads served with lightly spiced Bengal gram curry.

Ingredients: Maida, Bengal gram (chana dal), ginger, cumin, turmeric, salt and oil.

Recipe: 1. Knead flour with water and a little oil to make a smooth dough.
2. Soak and boil the Bengal gram until tender.
3. Cook ginger and spices in a pan.
4. Add the cooked dal and simmer until thick.
5. Roll small dough circles and deep-fry the luchi until puffed.

Pakhala Bhata

Origin: Odisha
A traditional rice dish served with water or lightly fermented rice water and accompaniments.

Ingredients: Cooked rice, clean drinking water, salt, curd (optional) and green chillies.

Recipe: 1. Cook rice and let it cool. 2. Place it in a clean bowl and add drinking water.
3. Add a little salt and curd if desired.
4. Refrigerate for a short time for a chilled version, or follow a traditional fermentation method with proper food-safety precautions.
5. Serve with roasted vegetables, greens or fried fish.

Litti Chokha

Origin: Bihar
Roasted wheat dough balls stuffed with spiced roasted gram flour, served with mashed vegetables.

Ingredients: Wheat flour, roasted gram flour (sattu), mustard oil, ajwain, onion, green chillies, eggplant and tomatoes.

Recipe: 1. Make a firm dough with wheat flour, water and a little oil.
2. Mix sattu with spices, chopped onion, chillies and mustard oil.
3. Fill dough balls with the sattu mixture and seal.
4. Bake or roast until golden and cooked through.
5. Roast eggplant and tomatoes, then mash with onion, coriander and seasonings to make chokha.
6. Serve the litti with chokha and a little ghee.

Dhuska

Popular in Jharkhand
A crispy fried snack made from a rice-and-lentil batter.

Ingredients: Rice, chana dal, urad dal, green chillies, ginger, cumin and salt.

Recipe: 1. Soak rice and lentils for several hours.
2. Grind them into a thick batter with ginger and chillies.
3. Add cumin and salt.
4. Pour small portions into hot oil.
5. Fry until golden and cooked through.
