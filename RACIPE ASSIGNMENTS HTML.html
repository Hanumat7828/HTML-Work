FAVOURITE RECIPE

Making Samosa

~Ingredients~

  • Maida – 2 cup
  • Aloo – 4 medium
  • Green chilli – 1–2
  • Ginger – 1 tsp
  • Coriander – thoda
  • Jeera – ½ tsp
  • Dhaniya powder – 1 tsp
  • Garam masala – ½ tsp
  • Amchur – ½ tsp
  • Salt – taste ke according
  • Oil – frying ke liye

~Method~

  1. Dough:
    2. Maida me salt + 4 tbsp oil daalo. Achhe se mix karke thoda-thoda water daal kar tight dough bana lo. 20 min rest do.
  2. Filling:
    3. Aloo boil karke mash karo. Pan me thoda oil, jeera, ginger aur green chilli daalo. Phir aloo, dhaniya powder, garam masala, amchur aur salt daal kar 2–3 min bhuno.
  3. Samosa shape:
    4. Dough ki small ball banao → oval shape me belo → beech se cut karo → cone banao → filling bharo → edges ko water se seal karo.
  4. Fry:
    5. Oil ko medium-low heat par rakho aur samose slowly fry karo. Golden brown aur crispy hone tak fry karo.

~Conclusion~

Crispy samose ka secret: Dough tight rakho aur samose ko bahut tez garam oil me mat daalo. Garam samose hari chutney + meethi imli chutney ke saath serve karo.
