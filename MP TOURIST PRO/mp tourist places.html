1. KHAJURAHO

The Khajuraho Group of Monuments are a group of Hindu and Digambara Jain temples in Chhatarpur district, Madhya Pradesh, India. They are about 46 km (28.6 mi) from Chhatarpur city, the district headquarters, 283 km (177 mi) from Gwalior,
175 kilometres (109 mi) southeast of Jhansi, 10 kilometres (6.2 mi) from Khajwa and 9 kilometres (5.6 mi) from Rajnagar. The temples are famous for their Nagara-style architectural symbolism and a few erotic sculptures.

    • Around 12,000 according to the 2011 Census.

  • ~ Famous Food ~

    • Poha, Pakora, Samosa, Jalebi and local vegetarian dishes.

    Transportation ~ Buses, taxis and private vehicles. The nearest major railway station is Pipariya.

    • Great Stupa , Stupa No. 2 , Stupa No. 3 , Sanchi Museum , Ashoka Pillar and Buddhist Monuments.

  • ~ Population ~

    • Around 8,500 according to the 2011 Census.

  • ~ Famous Food ~

  • ~ Population ~

    • Around 10,000

  • ~ Famous Food ~

    • Dal Bafla, Poha, Kachori, Samosa and Jalebi.

  • ~ Location & Transpotation ~

    • Location ~ Niwari District, Madhya Pradesh
    Transportation ~ Buses and taxis are commonly used. Jhansi is the nearest major railway connection.

It is part of the Kanha Tiger Reserve and is famous for its forests, wildlife and natural landscapes.

    • Kanha Zone , Kisli Zone , Khatia Gate , Wildlife Safari , Forest landscapes and Bamni Dadar viewpoint.

  • ~ Population ~

    • Not applicable to the national park itself because it is a protected forest area.

  • ~ Famous Food ~

    • Poha, Dal Bafla, Samosa and local vegetarian food.

  • ~ Location & Transpotation ~

    • Location ~ Mandla and Balaghat Districts, Madhya Pradesh
    Transportation ~ Road and taxis are commonly used. Jabalpur and Gondia provide nearby air/rail connections.
